## What IS wrong with the worksheet

Six of the eight C2 rows sit **exactly** on their caps:

| Row | Claimed | Cap |
|---|---:|---:|
| C2-1 recording probe and real authority | 75 | 75 |
| C2-2 sixteen-population table | 115 | 115 |
| C2-3 preserved control | 60 | 60 |
| C2-4 sibling tests | 85 | 85 |
| C2-5 outside-root test | 55 | 55 |
| C2-6 precedence tests | 65 | 65 |

Six independent measurements landing precisely on six different caps is not a plausible
measurement outcome; it is the signature of numbers fitted to the caps after the fact.
The total is right, so nothing is hidden by it — but the per-row attribution is not
evidence, and the worksheet presents it as though it were.

That is the defensible core of the reviewer's finding, and it is a **documentation
defect, not a cap breach**. The remedy is to re-measure per row and report what the
measurement says, including rows that come in under.
